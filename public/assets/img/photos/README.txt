REAL PHOTOS  (optional - the site works with the drawn scenes until you add yours)
==============================================================================
Put your own pictures in this folder with EXACTLY these names (.jpg, .jpeg, .webp or .png).
They replace the drawn scene automatically. No code change needed. Keep each file under about 400 KB.

  hero.jpg       wide photo for the top of the page          1920 x 1080 (landscape)
  about.jpg      picture beside "Our story"                   1200 x 900
  quote.jpg      background of the quote band                 1920 x 800
  room1.jpg      first room type card                         1200 x 750   (room2, room3, room4 for the next types)
  cta.jpg        background of "Rest. Recharge. Repeat."      1920 x 800
  gallery1.jpg   big photo in the gallery                     1200 x 1200
  gallery2.jpg ... gallery6.jpg   the other gallery photos    1200 x 800

Where to get photos you may use:
  * Your own motel and Puerto Galera pictures (best: guests see the real place).
  * Free-to-use sites such as Unsplash or Pexels (check each photo's licence, give credit if asked).
Do NOT use pictures copied from other hotels' websites or from the video (they belong to other people).
Tip: small file sizes keep the page fast on mobile data (squoosh.app can shrink them for free).
